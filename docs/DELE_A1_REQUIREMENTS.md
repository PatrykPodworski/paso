# Spanish DELE A1: requirements and preparation map

Research checked **7 September 2026**. Scope: **general DELE A1**, using the format introduced in 2020. The school examination has its own materials. This report separates the official assessment requirements from Paso’s original teaching choices.

## What passing means

Each skill contributes up to 25 points. The two required groups are:

- **Reading + writing: at least 30 out of 50.**
- **Listening + speaking: at least 30 out of 50.**

Both conditions must hold. A combined total of 60 is insufficient if one group is below 30. There is no requirement to score exactly 15 in each individual skill: performance can compensate within a group. For example, 20 reading + 10 writing and 12 listening + 18 speaking meet the group thresholds. A score of 25 + 25 in the first group and 5 + 5 in the second does not.

Source: [Instituto Cervantes — examination and passing rules](https://examenes.cervantes.es/es/dele/como).

## Exam format

| Component | Time                                                | Tasks and questions                                    | Points |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------ |
| Reading   | 45 minutes                                          | Four tasks, 25 items: 5 + 6 + 6 + 8                    | 25     |
| Listening | 25 minutes                                          | Four tasks, 25 items: 5 + 5 + 8 + 7                    | 25     |
| Writing   | 25 minutes                                          | Personal form, 15–25 words; short message, 30–40 words | 25     |
| Speaking  | 10 minutes, with another 10 minutes for preparation | Introduction; familiar topic; conversation             | 25     |

Speaking task targets are 1–2, 2–3 and 3–4 minutes respectively. Candidates prepare the first two tasks. The conversation includes answering follow-up questions and asking the interviewer two questions on the relevant topic. The centre supplies the actual appointment details.

Reading uses personal correspondence, notice matching, advertisement matching and practical information. Listening uses short conversations with visual choices, messages matched to visuals, sentence matching and a conversation with gap completion. The official recordings play each text twice. Correct objective answers earn one point; errors do not incur a deduction.

Writing tasks have equal weight. Productive responses use trained human raters and rating bands, scaled to 25. Understandable communication and fulfillment of the requested task matter; perfection is not expected.

Source: [Official A1 guide, 2020 format](https://examenes.cervantes.es/sites/default/files/DELE_A1_v2020_Gu%C3%ADa%20de%20examen.pdf), particularly the structure, task descriptions and rating sections. Use the actual rubric for detailed rater weighting and band descriptors.

## The language abilities behind the tasks

The A1 reference inventories cover grammar, pronunciation, orthography, communicative functions, pragmatic strategies, text types, general notions and specific everyday notions. The pages often show **A1 and A2 in adjacent columns**. Read the A1 entries for A1 requirements.

This is a proficiency examination: the inventories describe a range of usable language. They do not create a guaranteed-pass memorization list or a separate grammar paper. The [complete curriculum index](https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/indice.htm) is the authoritative way to inspect every sub-entry; the sections below provide a practical preparation checklist.

### Communicative functions

Prepare to introduce yourself and others; exchange identity and contact information; describe familiar people, possessions and places; ask straightforward questions; express a preference, need or simple reason; make requests; accept or decline a simple invitation; greet, thank, apologize and close an exchange. Practise asking someone to repeat, slow down, spell a name or explain a word. Basic turn-taking and polite formulas help sustain an exchange with a cooperative speaker.

Source: [Cervantes A1–A2 functions inventory, A1 column](https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/niveles/05_funciones_inventario_a1-a2.htm). Related pragmatic and discourse inventories are linked from the [curriculum index](https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/indice.htm).

### Grammar and sentence construction

Work through noun gender and number; adjective agreement and position; articles and basic article omission; _al_ and _del_; subject pronouns and forms of address; demonstratives and possessives; cardinal and basic ordinal numbers; quantifiers; basic question words; negation; and frequent time/place expressions.

Build short sentences with regular present-tense endings, basic common irregular forms, _ser_, _estar_, _hay_ and _tener_. Recognize useful reflexive patterns and elementary _gustar_ constructions. Use infinitives for activities, common prepositions and simple connections such as _y_, _pero_ and _porque_. Distinguish identity, state, location and existence in context. Treat the full inventory as a reference for the exact scope of subcategories and regional variants.

Source: [Cervantes grammar inventory, A1 column](https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/niveles/02_gramatica_inventario_a1-a2.htm).

### Everyday topics and vocabulary

Build usable vocabulary around identity and personal details; appearance, personality and feelings; family and relationships; home, rooms and furniture; education, work and routines; leisure and cultural activities; food and restaurants; clothing and shopping; transport and accommodation; local services, signs and directions; numbers, prices, dates and schedules; weather and simple physical needs.

Also consult the full inventories for general concepts such as quantity, time, position, quality and evaluation, and the less frequent A1 entries within broader topic families. A short app word bank is a starting set, not the whole linguistic inventory.

Sources: [Specific notions, A1 column](https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/niveles/09_nociones_especificas_inventario_a1-a2.htm), [general notions](https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/niveles/08_nociones_generales_inventario_a1-a2.htm).

### Sound, spelling and comprehensibility

Practise the five vowels, common consonant contrasts, syllable structure, stress, rhythm, pauses and the intonation of statements and questions. Pay attention to silent _h_, the distinction between _n_ and _ñ_, and single versus multiple _r_. Use audio models and listen back to your own speech. Intelligibility is the practical goal.

Source: [Cervantes pronunciation and prosody inventory](https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/niveles/03_pronunciacion_inventario_a1-a2.htm).

Learn to spell personal details and use Spanish letters, written accents, capitals, numbers and punctuation, including opening question and exclamation marks. Accents and _ñ_ can distinguish different words. In Paso, constrained typing accepts punctuation and capitalization differences and listed alternative answers while preserving those meaningful spelling distinctions.

Source: [Cervantes spelling inventory](https://cvc.cervantes.es/ensenanza/biblioteca_ele/plan_curricular/niveles/04_ortografia_inventario_a1-a2.htm). The described typing policy is Paso’s own practice design.

## Registration and exam-day requirements

1. Choose general DELE A1 and an authorized examination centre. A prior lower-level diploma is not a prerequisite.
2. Verify the centre’s current dates, registration deadline, payment method and fee. These vary; this report does not assume a particular country or session.
3. Supply the requested identification and registration information, complete payment and keep the registration receipt. Confirm how the centre sends your summons.
4. Check the written and oral appointments: location, date and time. Follow the centre’s instructions about arrival and writing materials.
5. Bring the original official photo identification/passport used for registration, registration receipt and official summons. Confirm the currently applicable identity instructions with the centre.
6. Arrange any required access accommodations with the centre during registration, using the applicable documentation and procedure.
7. Familiarize yourself with the official answer sheets. Use the designated response spaces, respond to all prompts and manage your time.

Sources: [Registration](https://examenes.cervantes.es/es/dele/inscribirse), [candidate FAQs](https://examenes.cervantes.es/es/dele/preguntas-frecuentes), [official preparation materials and answer sheets](https://examenes.cervantes.es/es/dele/preparar-prueba). Consult these live sources and the centre for changes to logistics.

## How Paso implements the research

The following learning sequence, original questions and teaching explanations are our instructional design; they are not an official Cervantes syllabus or a reproduction of its papers.

| Unit | Theme                       | Main application                                  |
| ---- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Greetings and first contact | Identity, _ser_, _llamarse_, clarification        |
| 2    | Personal details            | Age, nationality, numbers, contact information    |
| 3    | Family and friends          | Possessives, descriptions, agreement              |
| 4    | Home                        | Rooms, _hay_ / _estar_, spatial relations         |
| 5    | Daily life                  | Present tense, reflexives, times and schedules    |
| 6    | Food and cafés              | Requests, menus, prices, _gustar_                 |
| 7    | Neighborhood                | Public services, signs, directions                |
| 8    | Shopping                    | Clothes, colors, demonstratives, payment          |
| 9    | Free time                   | Hobbies, work, studies, invitations and reasons   |
| 10   | Travel                      | Tickets, hotels, destinations and announcements   |
| 11   | Weather and feelings        | Weather expressions, physical needs, appointments |
| 12   | Integration                 | Exam communication, spelling, detail and negation |

Each unit contains vocabulary/listening, grammar, real-life reading/listening, and sentence-building/writing/speaking lessons. This gives **48 lessons and 264 learning-path exercises**. The studio adds **24 foundation checks, six illustrated questions and a personal form**. The rehearsal adds **55 questions and productive tasks**, for **350 original exercises and tasks in total**. The app also includes 96 vocabulary cards and 169 locally bundled Spanish speech clips.

Errors in objective questions receive a correction and explanation. They are saved immediately to a review queue; an unassisted correct retry clears them. A revealed transcript remains flagged as assistance even when hidden again. Practice mixes prioritize untried and older questions. Progress, preferences, drafts, self-assessment checks and rehearsal state are stored locally.

Writing receives a model, word-count guidance, a few explicit pattern checks and a self-review checklist. Those checks are intentionally limited and do not claim to correct every possible error. Speaking supports recording, playback, download and reflection. A transcript is not generated and pronunciation is not automatically graded. Use a teacher or conversation partner to evaluate productive performance and spontaneous interaction.

The rehearsal mirrors the official item distributions and section times, including oral preparation. Its short controllable clips, visual symbols, per-question playback limits and browser navigation differ from the live examination. Match tasks offer the relevant shared options on each question; they do not reproduce a printed answer sheet. Use [official papers, recordings, transcripts and keys](https://examenes.cervantes.es/es/dele/preparar-prueba) for exact-format practice.

The app computes objective practice results and demonstrates the two-group rule. XP, streaks, checklist ticks and lesson completion are learning indicators, not certification or a prediction of passing. Writing/speaking scores must come from human assessment, or be clearly understood as hypothetical calculator inputs.
