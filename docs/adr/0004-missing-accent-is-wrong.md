# 0004. A missing written accent makes a typed answer wrong

Written accents and ñ are part of the Spanish the learner must produce, so a typed answer that misses or adds an accent is wrong, in every exercise. The checker ignores case, spacing, and punctuation such as ¿ ? ¡ !. When accents are the only difference from an accepted answer, the feedback says so and shows that answer, instead of the generic explanation. ñ never counts as an accented n, and porque, por que and por qué are different answers, not accent variants. The [#613](https://github.com/PatrykPodworski/paso/issues/613) specification proposed accepting a missing accent with a soft note; that was declined.
