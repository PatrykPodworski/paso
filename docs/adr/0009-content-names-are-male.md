# 0009. Content names are male

The recorded voice is male, so every person named in course and practice content has a male name, and the Spanish and English around the name agree with it (él, cansado, he). Names stay unique within an exercise set, so answers stay clear. The live-talk partner Lucía and her voice are not content and stay as they are.
