# 0011. An accent or ¿ ? mistake passes with a note and joins the mistake review

A typed answer that differs from an accepted answer only by its acute accents or diaeresis, or a correct answer to a full Spanish question that leaves out ¿ ?, counts as correct for the score, lesson completion and progress, and its feedback names the mistake as [0010](0010-accent-only-answer-passes-with-a-note.md) describes. The question also joins the mistake review, because a learner who sees the note once tends to repeat the slip ("esta" for "está"), and the DELE A1 exam takes only a little for it, under language correctness in writing. The question leaves the mistake review by the usual rule: an unassisted answer that is correct and has no note. The LLM review follows the same rule: it lists an accent or ¿ ? as a correction that does not lower the result, code tags each correction that changes only accents or ¿ ?, and a passed review whose every correction is tagged joins the mistake review. A partly passed review keeps its current rule. Flashcards are self-rated with no typed answer, so nothing changes there. The checker rules of 0010 stand: ñ is not an accented n, spacing differences are wrong, and chosen options and word tiles stay strict. This supersedes 0010; the decision is recorded in [#659](https://github.com/PatrykPodworski/paso/issues/659).
